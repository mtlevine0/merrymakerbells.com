Module adapter

Keep the songs on your old cartridge.

The module adapter joins your original song cartridge to a Merrymaker. Merrymaker listens while the cartridge plays, and saves each song to My songs, so you can play it on any set.

Connect them

Slide your original cartridge onto the end marked ORIGINAL MODULE, and Merrymaker onto the end marked MERRYMAKER. Keep the side that says THIS SIDE UP facing up.

On the board

  • ORIGINAL MODULE and MERRYMAKER mark which end takes which.
  • THIS SIDE UP on the front and THIS SIDE DOWN on the back.
  • SER: a box to write the adapter's serial number in.
  • The QR code that brought you here.

Questions

Does it change my cartridge?

No. Merrymaker only listens to what the cartridge plays. Nothing is written to it, and it goes back in your bells exactly as it was.
