Setup guide

From the box to ringing bells.

It takes about five minutes, and you only do it once. After that, your bells are a tap away whenever your phone is on your home Wi-Fi.

  • Your caroling bellsWith their song cartridge slot
  • Your MerrymakerAnd the power adapter it came with
  • Your phoneiPhone or Android, no app needed
  • Your Wi-Fi passwordFor a 2.4 GHz network

Swap the cartridge

Unplug your bells. Slide the old song cartridge out of the control box, and keep it somewhere safe. Then slide the Merrymaker into the same slot, the way it sits in this photo, until it's snug.

Plug in the new adapter

Power your bells with the adapter that came with Merrymaker, not the original one. Merrymaker needs a little more power than the old adapter can give.

After a few seconds the NET light starts blinking. That means it's ready to meet your phone.

Join the Merrymaker network

On your phone, open your Wi-Fi settings. You'll see a new network called Merrymaker- followed by four letters or numbers. Tap it. There's no password.

A Merrymaker page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open your web browser and go to 192.168.4.1.

Your phone may warn that this network has no internet. That's expected. Choose to stay connected.

Choose your home Wi-Fi

Pick your home network from the list, type its password and tap Join network. When Merrymaker is on, the NET light stops blinking and stays on, and the page says All set.

  • Tap Copy address, then Finish. Your phone goes back to your home Wi-Fi.
  • Open Safari or Chrome, paste the address and go. It looks like merrymaker-4f2a.local.

Tip: once the page is open, add it to your home screen. The Merrymaker bell shows up next to your apps, and opens your bells in one tap. The page shows you how.

Pick a song

Tap a collection, tap a song, and let them ring. Choose to play one song, repeat it, play through the whole collection or shuffle.

Got more than one set? Set each one up the same way, on the same Wi-Fi. Then press play on any of them, and they all ring together. You'll find every set listed under Choir.

If something's not right.

I can't see the Merrymaker network
  • Check the NET light is blinking. If it's on steadily, your set is already on Wi-Fi: press the WIFI button to open the network again.
  • If no lights come on at all, check the adapter is plugged in at both ends and the Merrymaker is pushed fully into its slot.
  • Give it 30 seconds after plugging in, then refresh the Wi-Fi list on your phone.
My home network isn't in the list

Merrymaker uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, the kind almost every home router has. If your router splits its networks by band, choose the one that doesn't end in "5G". Move the bells closer to the router if the network is weak.

It couldn't join my Wi-Fi

Usually the password was mistyped. If Merrymaker can't join, the Merrymaker network comes back by itself after about 20 seconds. Join it again and re-enter the password. Passwords are case-sensitive.

The address won't open
  • Make sure your phone is back on your home Wi-Fi, not the Merrymaker network or mobile data.
  • Try the number address shown under All set instead, such as 192.168.1.42. Some phones and computers don't understand the .local name.
  • Lost the address? Press the WIFI button, join the Merrymaker network, and the page shows it again.
The bells are quiet, or some bells don't ring
  • Use the adapter that came with Merrymaker. The original one doesn't give enough power.
  • Open Bells on the Merrymaker page and tap each bell to test it. A bell that never rings may need its hammer or plug checked on the bells themselves.
My sets aren't playing together

Each set needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Open Choir on any set's page to see which sets it can find. Guest networks and mesh systems that keep devices apart can stop sets from hearing each other.

I want to move it to a new router or a friend's house

Press the WIFI button, join the Merrymaker network and pick the new network. Or hold the WIFI button for 5 seconds to forget the old network and start setup again.
